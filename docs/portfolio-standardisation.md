The dissertation now uses a dedicated research renderer, documented in `dissertation-story.md`. The approved Discus renderer, data, CSS and interaction components are unchanged. Both renderers reuse its chapter navigation, section typography and next-project navigation.

Technical content comes from the existing `app/data/projects.ts` records. Source paragraphs are reused where appropriate; added contribution, outcome and competency summaries stay within those records. UAV performance figures remain targets and testing remains planned. FEA has no invented solver, loading conditions, mesh, preferred configuration or numerical results. The Teams research remains a proposal, with its sampling and implementation limitations preserved.

The two supplied Shimmy Damper photographs are placed within contribution and manufacturing chapters; no substitute engineering media is generated for projects without supplied assets.

Archive cards, global styles and the homepage retain their existing content and layout. Each detail route has its own engineering chapter sequence and next-project link.
